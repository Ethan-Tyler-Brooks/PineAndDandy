(01) — The big one

Bring the boat
& the whole crew.

The Grandy Dandy is built for gathering — a light, open-concept great room with a vaulted pine ceiling, a bubble chandelier over a big butcher-block table, buffalo-check chairs, and a wood stove for the cold nights.

Downstairs is the fun: a game room with a pool table, a Pac-Man arcade, and a bar. Out back, a cedar barrel sauna and a hot tub wait under the pines.

And the water's the headline — a private dock onto the boatable Sugar Camp Chain, five lakes you can explore all day.

(07) — The lake

Five lakes,
one chain.

The Grandy Dandy sits on the boatable Sugar Camp Chain of Lakes — a full recreational chain with a private dock right out back. Launch your own boat at the public ramp nearby off County Highway D.

The fishing's the real draw: walleye, musky, northern pike, largemouth & smallmouth bass, and panfish. Bring the kayaks and paddleboards out to explore the quiet channels.

Good to know

The Grandy Dandy, answered

Where is The Grandy Dandy located?
It’s at 4731 Grand View Drive in Eagle River, Wisconsin, on the Sugar Camp Chain of Lakes — about 15 minutes from downtown Eagle River.
How many guests does it sleep?
The Grandy Dandy sleeps 10 across 3 bedrooms, with 5 beds and 2 bathrooms, plus a basement futon for extra sleeping space.
Can I bring a boat?
Yes. It sits on the boatable Sugar Camp Chain — a five-lake chain — with a private dock right out back. There’s a public launch nearby off County Highway D. Two kayaks and two paddleboards are provided.
Does it have a sauna and hot tub?
Yes — an authentic cedar barrel sauna that fits up to 6 and a year-round hot tub that seats 5, both steps from the lower-level walkout. The basement also has a game room with a pool table, a Pac-Man arcade and a bar.
What&rsquo;s the fishing like on the Sugar Camp Chain?
The chain is a real draw for anglers — walleye, musky, northern pike, largemouth and smallmouth bass, and panfish.
Is it dog-friendly, and what are the check-in times?
Up to two dogs are welcome with prior approval (a large crate is available). Self check-in is via keypad after 4 PM, with check-out by 10 AM.
