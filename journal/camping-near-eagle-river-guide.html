Camping near the Northwoods

Where to pitch a tent or park an RV around Eagle River, when to go, and how it compares to a night at the cabin.

JournalArea GuideWhere to EatFishingThe Cabins

Ask us where to pitch a tent around Eagle River and we'll admit something first: with a lake cabin already stocked with real beds and a hot tub, most of our guests never sleep in one here. But plenty of people love camping in the Northwoods, and the country around us — national forest, county parks, private campgrounds — genuinely delivers. Here's what we'd tell a friend who wanted to camp near Eagle River.

The basics

Where can you camp near Eagle River?

Vilas County sits inside the Chequamegon-Nicolet National Forest, so public camping options are everywhere once you start looking. Add a handful of county parks and private campgrounds close to town, and there's a site for whatever kind of trip you're planning — quiet and rustic, or full hookups with a bathhouse and a camp store.

National forest campgrounds

The Nicolet's rustic campgrounds are scattered around area lakes — vault toilets, a hand pump for water, and not much else besides trees and quiet. Many run first-come, first-served, so arrive early on a summer weekend.

County & town options

Vilas County parks and a few sites closer to town offer a step up in convenience, often near a boat launch or beach, with a little more structure than a forest site.

Private campgrounds & RV parks

Private campgrounds around Eagle River add showers, electric and water hookups, and sometimes a pool or camp store — worth it if you're pulling a camper or traveling as a crowd.

Tent or trailer

Tent camping or pulling in an RV?

Both work well here. Tent campers tend to gravitate toward the national forest sites for the quiet and the price; RVs and camper trailers usually head for private campgrounds with hookups, since forest sites rarely offer electricity. Either way, book ahead for summer holiday weekends — the Fourth of July and early August fill up fast around the chain — and keep some cash on hand, since plenty of rustic sites and self-pay stations don't take cards.

Timing

When's the best time to camp in the Northwoods?

The camping season here really runs from late May through September, with June through August the busiest — and buggiest — stretch. If you can swing it, the shoulder weeks, late May or September into early October, bring cooler nights, thinner crowds, and, come fall, color starting to turn right over the campsite. Pack layers regardless of the month; Northwoods nights cool off fast even in midsummer, and can dip near freezing by September.

Before you go

A few things every Northwoods camper should know

Bugs

Black flies and mosquitoes peak in June. Pack repellent and a head net if you're sensitive, especially near water at dawn and dusk.

Campfires

Burn restrictions can go into effect during dry stretches. Check current conditions before you count on a fire, and always keep water close by.

Food storage

Black bears live throughout the Northwoods. Keep food, coolers, and anything scented sealed in a vehicle overnight, not in your tent.

Cell service

Coverage gets spotty the deeper you go into the forest. Download offline maps and let someone know your general plans before heading out.

Good to know

Common questions

Are there campgrounds near Eagle River?
Yes. Vilas County sits inside the Chequamegon-Nicolet National Forest, so there are rustic forest campgrounds scattered around area lakes, plus county parks and private campgrounds closer to town with more amenities.
What's the best time of year to camp near Eagle River?
The season generally runs late May through September, with summer the busiest stretch. Late May and September bring cooler nights and thinner crowds, plus fall color moving in by late September.
Should we camp or stay in a cabin near Eagle River?
It depends what you're after. Camping is affordable and puts you right in the woods; a lakefront cabin trades the tent for real beds, a hot tub, and a private dock while keeping the same firepit-and-stars evenings. Both Pine & Dandy cabins are built for exactly that trade.
