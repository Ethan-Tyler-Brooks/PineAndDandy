Good to know

Frequently asked

Where are the cabins located?
Both cabins are in Eagle River, in Wisconsin’s Northwoods. They sit on two different lakes a short drive apart — The O.G. on Lake Arbutus and The Grandy Dandy on the Sugar Camp Chain.
How many cabins are there and how many guests can they hold?
Two cabins. The O.G. sleeps 8 across 4 bedrooms; The Grandy Dandy sleeps 10 across 3 bedrooms.
Which cabin should I choose?
Pick The O.G. for a quiet, no-wake lake, a sandy beach and smaller groups. Pick The Grandy Dandy if you want to bring a boat, gather a bigger crew, and have a barrel sauna and game room.
Are the cabins dog-friendly?
Yes — up to two dogs are welcome at each cabin with prior approval.
Are the cabins open year-round?
Yes, both cabins are available year-round, with a hot tub at each for cold-weather stays.
How do I book?
Each cabin has its own booking page — open The O.G. or The Grandy Dandy and use the secure reservation calendar to check dates and book.
