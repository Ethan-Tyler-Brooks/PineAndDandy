(01) — Where it began

A log cabin
with a story.

The O.G. is the cabin that started it all — a hand-hewn Lincoln Log cabin with full log walls and a vaulted log ceiling, tucked above the still water of private, spring-fed Lake Arbutus.

Climb to the open loft primary with a king bed and a private balcony over the trees. Downstairs, a vintage gallery wall, a teal kitchen, and a wrap-around deck with a firepit and a year-round hot tub strung with lights.

It's the quiet one: a no-wake lake, a little sandy beach, and the kind of hush you only find at the end of a private drive.

Lake Arbutus is a peaceful 58-acre no-wake lake — no launch, no jet-ski hum, just clear spring water, a little sandy beach, and a swim platform.

Bring the kayaks out at sunrise. The dock and water toys are in from roughly May through November.

Good to know

The O.G., answered

Where is The O.G. located?
It’s at 4281 Red Pine Drive in Eagle River, Wisconsin, on private Lake Arbutus — about 10 minutes from downtown Eagle River.
How many guests does it sleep?
The O.G. sleeps 8 across 4 bedrooms, with 6 beds and 2 bathrooms.
Is Lake Arbutus a no-wake lake?
Yes. Lake Arbutus is a quiet, spring-fed 58-acre no-wake lake with no public launch — no jet skis or speedboats. The cabin has its own sandy beach and a swim platform, plus two kayaks, a paddle boat and a rowboat (in the water roughly May through November).
Is there a hot tub?
Yes — a year-round hot tub on the wrap-around deck, strung with lights, plus a firepit with Adirondack chairs and free firewood.
Is The O.G. dog-friendly?
Yes, up to two dogs with prior approval. A large crate is available.
What are the check-in and check-out times?
Self check-in via smart lock any time after 4 PM; check-out is by 10 AM. Your door code arrives before you come.
